Artificial intelligence is a field of computer science that focuses on creating systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. These tasks include learning reasoning problem solving language understanding and decision making. Artificial intelligence systems can use large amounts of data to identify patterns and produce useful predictions. Machine learning is one of the major areas of artificial intelligence. Modern artificial intelligence is used in recommendation systems virtual assistants fraud detection medical analysis and autonomous machines. The development of artificial intelligence requires algorithms data computing resources and careful evaluation. Researchers also study fairness safety privacy and reliability because intelligent systems can affect important decisions. Artificial intelligence continues to develop as computers become more powerful and more data becomes available.
