Renewable energy comes from sources that are naturally replenished over time. Solar energy wind energy hydropower biomass and geothermal energy are common renewable sources. Solar photovoltaic systems convert sunlight directly into electrical energy using semiconductor materials. Wind turbines convert the movement of air into electricity. Renewable energy can reduce dependence on fossil fuels and can help reduce greenhouse gas emissions. Solar power systems are affected by sunlight temperature shading and the properties of photovoltaic materials. Energy storage can help provide electricity when renewable generation is not available. Batteries are commonly used for storage in smaller systems while other technologies can support larger energy systems. Renewable energy research focuses on improving efficiency reducing cost increasing reliability and developing better materials and energy storage methods.
