Natural language processing is a field of artificial intelligence that focuses on enabling computers to work with human language. Applications include text classification sentiment analysis machine translation question answering information retrieval and speech related systems. Text preprocessing is an important step in many natural language processing tasks. Common preprocessing methods include converting text to a consistent case removing unnecessary punctuation tokenization removing stop words and stemming or lemmatization. Tokenization divides text into smaller units such as words. Stemming reduces words to a common root form while lemmatization attempts to produce a meaningful base word. Machine learning and deep learning models can learn relationships between words and sentences. Natural language processing is widely used in search engines chat systems document analysis customer support and language translation.
