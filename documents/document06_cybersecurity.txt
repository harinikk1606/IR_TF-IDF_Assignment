Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers networks applications and data from unauthorized access damage and disruption. Cyber threats include malware phishing password attacks ransomware data theft and denial of service attacks. Strong authentication can reduce the risk of unauthorized access. Encryption protects information by converting readable data into a protected form. Firewalls can control network traffic and security monitoring can help identify suspicious activity. Software should be updated regularly because security updates can fix known vulnerabilities. Users also play an important role in cybersecurity by using strong passwords avoiding suspicious links and protecting sensitive information. Organizations often use security policies access controls backups and incident response plans. Cybersecurity is important for individuals companies governments hospitals banks and educational institutions because digital systems contain valuable information.
