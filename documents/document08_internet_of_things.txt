The Internet of Things describes a network of physical devices that can collect exchange and process data. These devices may contain sensors processors communication modules and software. Examples include smart watches smart home devices industrial sensors agricultural systems and connected vehicles. Sensors can measure temperature pressure motion light humidity and other physical conditions. The collected information can be sent to a local computer or cloud platform for analysis. IoT systems can support automation by allowing devices to respond to sensor measurements. Security is an important concern because connected devices can become targets for attackers. IoT applications are used in smart agriculture healthcare industrial automation transportation energy management and home automation. Combining IoT with artificial intelligence can allow systems to identify patterns and make predictions from sensor data.
