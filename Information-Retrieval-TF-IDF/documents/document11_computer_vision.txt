Computer vision is an area of artificial intelligence that enables computers to analyze and understand images and video. A computer vision system can identify objects detect faces classify images track movement and measure visual features. Image preprocessing may include resizing noise reduction normalization and color conversion. Feature extraction can identify useful patterns from images while modern deep learning models can learn visual features automatically. Convolutional neural networks have been widely used for image classification object detection and segmentation. Computer vision is applied in medical imaging autonomous vehicles security agriculture manufacturing and robotics. Image quality lighting camera position and training data can affect system performance. Computer vision systems often require large datasets and careful evaluation to ensure that predictions are accurate and reliable.
