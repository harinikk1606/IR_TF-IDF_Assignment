Robotics combines mechanical engineering electronics computer science and artificial intelligence to create machines that can sense plan and perform physical actions. A robot may use sensors to understand its environment motors to move and a controller to coordinate actions. Common sensors include cameras distance sensors accelerometers gyroscopes and temperature sensors. Robots can be controlled using predefined programs or intelligent algorithms that allow them to adapt to changing conditions. Computer vision can help robots identify objects and navigate environments. Robotics is used in manufacturing warehouses healthcare agriculture exploration and education. Autonomous robots need reliable perception planning control and safety mechanisms. Research in robotics also focuses on human robot interaction navigation manipulation and learning. Combining robotics with artificial intelligence can make machines more capable of performing complex tasks in changing environments.
