Information retrieval is the process of finding useful documents from a collection in response to a user query. A search system first processes documents and queries so that their words can be compared. Text preprocessing may include tokenization case normalization stop word removal and stemming. Term frequency measures how often a word occurs in a document. Inverse document frequency measures how important a word is across the entire document collection. The TF IDF method combines these measurements to give higher importance to terms that are frequent in a particular document but uncommon across the collection. A query can be represented using the same TF IDF process. Similarity between a query and documents can then be calculated using methods such as cosine similarity. Documents with higher similarity scores can be ranked above documents with lower scores.
